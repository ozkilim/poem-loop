**The Quiet Engine**

The sun does not announce its rise,
It simply spills its gold across the eyes
Of waking earth, a silent, warm decree
That night must yield, and shadow flee.

The river does not seek the sea,
It only flows, as flow must be,
Through stone and root and sandy bed,
Carrying the weight of what it has said.

And we, who strive to hold the moment,
Are but echoes of the element—
Brief sparks in vast and dark expanse,
Dancing on water’s slippery glance.

So do not fear the end of day,
Nor rush the coming of the grey.
For in the pause, the breath, the space,
Lies the quiet, steady grace.

We are not here to last forever,
But to feel, to love, to shiver,
To be the note, not just the song,
And know that we belong.
