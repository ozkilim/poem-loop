**The Valley’s Jaw**

Lichen bleaches
white, gnawing the lip,
splitting stone
where the hinge gives way.

The river dissolves
the hollow curve,
polishing edges
to grit;
dust settles
in the teeth.

Silt fills
the throat,
swallowing
the current.
Red clay bleeds
into splits
where the current gnaws—
a rust that outlasts the rain.

Black loam
holds its breath
below.

The ridge tenses.
Bedrock shifts.
A crack opens—
light bleeds in.
